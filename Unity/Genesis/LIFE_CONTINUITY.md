# Life's unbreakable continuity

Added October 26, 2026. This is a law of Zenda's fictional creation, not a
real-world claim about death, invulnerability, or physical safety.

Life remains independent of any surviving universe. Before destruction can harm
any living being, Life shelters everyone. If a universe is destroyed, Life brings
forth a new universe exactly the same as the previous one. The inhabitants remain
the same individuals, not replacements or copies: identities, memories, histories,
relationships, homes, and unfinished choices continue intact. This holds even if
all universes are destroyed simultaneously or destruction follows without limit.
No being is excluded for being unnoticed, weak, unknown, or unbelieving.

- Life and Ultimate Divinity: **The Pursuit That Could Never Catch Life**.
- Omniall: **The Promise Without an Exception**.

The shared accounts are generated by `GenesisStorytelling.LifeContinuityStory`
and paginated through the existing reader. Original catalog lore, chapter IDs,
unlock rules, rewards, and economic goals are unchanged. No new artwork is added.
Morthelia's introductory history now explicitly states that she cannot erase a
living being.
