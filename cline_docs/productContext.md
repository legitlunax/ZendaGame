# Product Context

## Why GenesisIdleRPG Exists
To provide a smooth, engaging cosmic idle clicker experience where player actions trigger generative cosmic visuals, offline calculations, prestige upgrades, and optional AI-assisted celestial lore.

## Key Workflows
- Quanta generation from generators (Quark Filament to Black Hole Anchors).
- Relic collection with seed-based ascension prestige.
- Timed divine miracles (Chrono-Weave, Supernova Burst).
- AI dialogue / lore generation via ChatGPT & MiniMax.
